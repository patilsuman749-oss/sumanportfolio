ScoreX 🏏

Every match. Every score. One place.

ScoreX is a match-scoring app where players and teams add scores as the game happens and keep every result organised in one place.

OVERVIEW

Scoring that stays
out of the way.

Scores are easy to lose track of when a match is moving fast. ScoreX gives every match a clear home, so the score is always one glance away.

Add the match, enter the score as it happens, and come back to the result whenever you need it. The interface is kept deliberately focused: fewer screens, bigger controls, nothing in the way of the game.

BRAND

The look
of the game.

A mark with momentum.

The logo pairs a set of stumps with a ball leaving a motion trail, next to a bold, wide wordmark. It is a strict black and white identity: high contrast, readable at a glance, and easy to recognise at small sizes.
